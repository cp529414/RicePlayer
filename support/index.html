Rice Player

Support

Need help with Rice Player or Rice Player Pro on Mac or Apple Vision Pro? Start with the topics below. To compare plans and prices, see Plans.

A video will not play

Playback depends on the file, its codecs, and your device. Rice Player opens MP4, MOV, and supported MKV files, and some formats need preparation before they play. Try another copy of the file and check that it is not damaged. If a video plays in another player but not in Rice Player, contact us with the container, video codec, and device model.

Subtitles do not appear

Choose a subtitle track from the captions control in the player. External subtitle files work best when they have the same name as the video and sit in a folder you have allowed Rice Player to read. If the text looks garbled, try another subtitle encoding when the app offers one.

Playback stopped for the day

The free edition plays up to 30 minutes in total each day. When the time is used up, playback stays paused until tomorrow. Rice Player Pro has no daily limit.

Restore or manage a purchase

In Rice Player Pro, open the purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases with the Apple Account you used to buy. To cancel or change a subscription, open Apple Account subscriptions. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

RiceNetwork cannot find the other device

RiceNetwork needs Rice Player Pro on both devices and the same local network. Check that sharing is active on the sending device and that both devices have allowed Local Network access. Compare the security code on both devices before you approve a connection.

Contact

For questions or bug reports, open an issue on the public GitHub repository. Tell us the app, your device and system version, and what happened. Keep personal information and private media out of public posts. See the Privacy Policy for how the apps handle your data.
