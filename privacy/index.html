Privacy Policy

Playback and local data

RicePlayer opens media and subtitles you choose. Playback progress, app preferences, and saved sharing settings are stored by the app on your device. RicePlayer does not require a RicePlayer account. Your videos are not sent to an advertising provider for banner ads.

RiceNetwork in Pro

When you use RiceNetwork, devices may exchange network addresses, device names, pairing information, and session data to discover and connect to one another. Approved receivers can access media, metadata, and subtitles you make available. You can stop sharing or remove saved connections in the app.

Advertising and privacy choices

When advertising is enabled in the free iPhone and iPad edition, Google Mobile Ads provides banner ads and Google's User Messaging Platform handles applicable consent choices. RicePlayer requests non-personalized ads and does not request Apple's tracking permission. Google may process IP addresses, device identifiers, advertising data, interactions, and diagnostics to provide and measure ads and prevent fraud. Open Privacy options in the app when that control is available.

See Google's SDK data disclosure and Google's privacy policy. Pro and the native Mac and Apple Vision Pro editions do not include Google's advertising SDK.

App Store purchases

Apple handles payments. RicePlayer uses App Store transaction and entitlement information to determine whether purchased features are available. See Apple's privacy policy.

Website hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting data and contacting the developer

You can remove saved connections in the app and delete the app to remove its local app data. Media stored outside the app remains in its original location. For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact on the App Store listing when available. The public GitHub repository is suitable for general questions. Keep personal information and private media out of public posts.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
