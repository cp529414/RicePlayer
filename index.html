RiceNetwork

Share a video on your local network

Open RicePlayer Pro on both devices and connect them to the same local network.

  1. On the sending device, open Share and add a compatible video.
  2. On the receiving device, open Receive and select the sender.
  3. Compare the security code on both devices before approving the connection. Choose the shared video on the receiving device.

If the sender is missing, check that sharing is active and that both devices have granted local network access where prompted.
