Free and Pro

Rice Player

Play local videos and use available audio tracks, subtitles, and playback controls. Videos play for up to 30 minutes in total each day; you can seek forward and back, and once the day's time is used up playback stays paused until tomorrow.

Rice Player Pro

Play without the daily playback limit, and access RiceNetwork features on supported devices. See the in-app purchase screen for any plan requirements and pricing for your platform.

RiceNetwork

Share a video on your local network

Open Rice Player Pro on both devices and connect them to the same local network.

  1. On the sending device, open Share and add a compatible video.
  2. On the receiving device, open Receive and select the sender.
  3. Compare the security code on both devices before approving the connection. Choose the shared video on the receiving device.

If the sender is missing, check that sharing is active and that both devices have granted local network access where prompted.
