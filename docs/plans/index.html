In the free edition you can still seek forward and back. Once the day's playback time is used up, playback stays paused until tomorrow.

Rice Player Pro plans

  • Monthly: US$1 per month, renewing automatically.
  • Lifetime: US$50, one time.

These are US base prices. The app shows the current price for your App Store storefront before you buy. A monthly subscription renews until you cancel it at least 24 hours before the period ends. A lifetime purchase gives the same access without renewal. Optional tips of US$10 or US$30 are one-time purchases that unlock nothing and cannot be restored. Apple handles billing and refund requests. See the Terms of Use.
