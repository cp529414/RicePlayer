Rice Player

Privacy Policy

This policy covers Rice Player and Rice Player Pro on every supported Apple platform. The same text is available inside each app.

Playback and local data

Rice Player and Rice Player Pro open media and subtitles you choose. Playback progress, app preferences, and saved sharing settings are stored by the app on your device. Neither app requires a Rice Player account.

RiceNetwork

RiceNetwork is part of Rice Player Pro. When you use RiceNetwork, devices may exchange network addresses, device names, pairing information, and session data to discover and connect to one another. Approved receivers can access media, metadata, and subtitles you make available. You can stop sharing or remove saved connections in the app.

Advertising

The free iPhone edition of Rice Player may show a banner advertisement on its main screen. Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform provide the advertising and the privacy choices that apply to you. Advertising providers may process device and advertising information, interaction data, diagnostics, and network information under their own policies. Rice Player requests non-personalized ads, does not request access to Apple's advertising identifier, and does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Where required, you can reopen your advertising privacy choices with Privacy options on the main screen. Your videos are not sent to advertising providers.

See Google's SDK data disclosure and Google's privacy policy. Rice Player Pro and the Mac and Apple Vision Pro editions do not include an advertising SDK.

App Store purchases

Apple handles payments, including optional tips. Rice Player Pro uses App Store transaction and entitlement information to determine whether purchased features are available. See Apple's privacy policy.

Website hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting data and contacting the developer

You can remove saved connections in the app and delete the app to remove its local app data. Media stored outside the app remains in its original location. For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact on the App Store listing when available. The public RicePlayer GitHub repository is suitable for general questions. Keep personal information and private media out of public posts.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on the Rice Player website.
